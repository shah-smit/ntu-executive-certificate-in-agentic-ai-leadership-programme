Dear Participant,

Welcome to the Future of AI Leadership! 

Artificial Intelligence is no longer a future ambition — it is an operating reality. Across industries, organisations are moving beyond experimentation to enterprise-wide AI deployment. In particular, agentic AI systems — capable of planning, orchestrating workflows, using tools and executing autonomously — are reshaping how work gets done.

As organisations expand their AI initiatives, leadership is evolving alongside technology. The next frontier lies in moving beyond individual tools to building structured, enterprise-wide agentic systems that deliver sustained impact. Leaders today have the opportunity to design intelligent workflows, embed AI into operating models and guide their organisations through this transformation with clarity and confidence.

The Executive Certificate in Agentic AI Leadership has been designed to equip you with the strategic frameworks, governance models and practical tools required to transition from AI experimentation to structured, enterprise execution. Over the next 18 weeks, you will develop the capability to lead Human + Agent teams, scale agentic AI initiatives and turn innovation into measurable business value.

By the end of this programme, you will be able to:

Explain the evolution and business implications of Agentic AI
Design and build no-code agentic workflows for real business use cases
Identify and prioritise high-impact Agentic AI opportunities across business functions
Plan, run and scale Agentic AI pilots into operational workflows
Apply governance, risk and accountability frameworks for AI agents
Lead the adoption of Human + Agent teams within the organisation
Demonstrate leadership as an Agentic AI decision-maker
In addition to high-quality pre-recorded lectures from NTU faculty, the programme features live, interactive sessions with faculty and industry experts. You will apply your learning through practical exercises, discussions, quizzes, assignments and a hands-on capstone project designed to address real business challenges.

This programme is more than an introduction to AI — it is a leadership journey designed to help you confidently shape the future of work in your organisation.

We wish you every success as you begin this transformative learning experience and take the next step in leading with Agentic AI!
